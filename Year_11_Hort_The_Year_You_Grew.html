🌱 HORT: THE YEAR YOU GREW

A 50-minute interactive reflection for Year 11 Horticulture

Mission briefing2 min

You've made it through a year of Horticulture.

This is not a boring "write about your year" worksheet. You are going to look back at what you actually learned, did, struggled with and improved — then decide what kind of grower you are becoming.

Relief instructions: Work independently. Type your answers into the activity. Be honest — this is about your learning, not giving the "right" answer. Complete every section.
Level 1 — Memory garden8 min

🧠 What actually stuck?

First, test your memory. Choose the best answer. You get immediate feedback.

Level 2 — Your growing season10 min

📸 Reconstruct your year

Think back to the practical work, theory, garden jobs, assessments, discussions and moments that stand out.

Level 3 — Skills audit8 min

🛠️ What can you do now that you couldn't do before?

Rate yourself honestly. There is no prize for pretending you are an expert.

Skill profile: Start rating your skills.

Level 4 — Horticultural judgement8 min

🌿 You're the grower now.

These are real-world decisions. There isn't always one perfect answer. Choose the option you think is most defensible, then explain your reasoning.

Scenario 1: Your vegetable plants are growing poorly. The first thing you should investigate is...

A. Immediately add lots of fertiliser
B. Soil, moisture, light and plant condition
C. Blame the seeds
D. Water them constantly
Scenario 2: You notice many different insects in a healthy garden. Your best interpretation is...

A. All insects are pests
B. Biodiversity can support a more resilient ecosystem
C. Spray everything
D. Remove the insects before they reproduce
Scenario 3: A crop fails. A good horticultural response is to...

A. Give up
B. Analyse the evidence, identify likely causes and adjust the next attempt
C. Repeat exactly the same process
D. Decide you are bad at growing
Level 5 — The grower's legacy9 min

🌻 What are you taking with you?

Imagine someone new joins Horticulture next year and asks you: "Is this subject worth doing?"

Mission complete

🏆 Your Year in Horticulture

—

Grower, your year was not just about plants.

Every grower learns by observing, trying, failing, adapting and trying again.

Final challenge: Take one minute to read your answers back. Is there evidence that you have grown this year — not just as a horticulture student, but as a problem-solver?
